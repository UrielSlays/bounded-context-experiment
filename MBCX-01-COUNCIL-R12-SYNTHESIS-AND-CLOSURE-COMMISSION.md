## 2. Governing disposition

This is not a thirteenth conceptual redesign. It is one closure commission against the r12 architecture.

The owner-adopted verdict taxonomy is:

- `GO`: the reviewed gate is ready to advance.
- `GO-WITH-FIXES`: the research question and architecture are accepted, and every remaining blocker is a bounded local repair that does not change the estimand, authority model, inference, sample, or stage structure.
- `NO-GO`: repair requires redesign, changes what is measured, invalidates inference, leaves authority unbounded, or makes practical decisiveness unknowable.

Under that taxonomy, r12 is `GO-WITH-FIXES`. The fixes below must close before the physical BUILD gate opens. This classification does not weaken fail-loud behavior: a reachable false green remains a mandatory repair, but a bounded wiring defect no longer resets the scientific design.

## 3. Closure law

> Integrity is not authority, and local validity is not cross-component consistency.

For every pair of artifacts that represent the same experiment truth, r13 SHALL define one canonical equality, one positive end-to-end witness, and one adversarial disagreement witness. If two independently valid objects can disagree without rejection, closure fails.

The complete matrix lives in `MBCX-01-R13-AUTHORITY-INVARIANT-MATRIX.md`. No new feature may be added to conceal a red row.
