## Frozen experiment

The eligible retained manifest contains exactly 768 globally unique scenarios: 128 from each of six motifs. [2026-08-16, owner-ratified, #23: motif content semantics are owned by the BUILD cohort design of record (MBCX-01-BUILD-COHORT-CONTENT-DESIGN.md); the frozen layer constrains the taxonomy to the six named motifs and their totals, not their content.] BC and OM share scenario, cut, observation, action universe, neutral schema, instructions, descriptions, padding, output schema, and transport settings. They differ only in how the object set is selected: BC uses the candidate's frozen public selector; OM uses the independent gold read-set.

Each condition has three scheduled model nodes (`A0`, `B`, `A1`), so the complete scientific run has 4,608 scheduled logical call positions. That number is not promised accepted responses or raw attempts. Retries, if the frozen route permits them, are separately capped before owner authorization.

There is one terminal scientific look at 768 complete or worst-case-resolved pairs. The family error is 0.05 across the delta and OM streams. The noninferiority margin is -0.10 and the OM floor is 0.80. The substantive branches retain the r8 meanings, but r10 makes no 90% operating-characteristic promise in the numerical transition region. Missing transport outcomes can produce `TRANSPORT_INCONCLUSIVE`; schema-invalid model output is observed failure, not missingness.

## Reliability before science

The route qualification unit is the complete six-call pair. Launch 456 representative independent route trials under the frozen endpoint, model snapshot, authentication path, transport settings, and retry policy. Pass at four or fewer failed pairs. Count every launched trial and classify failures by mode, position, or `OTHER_UNCLASSIFIED` for diagnosis. This gate controls acceptance of a 2%-bad route at 0.05 and passes a 0.5%-good route with probability at least 0.90.

No reliability soak and no scientific model call is authorized by this document. Prices, token ceilings, raw-attempt ceilings, endpoints, snapshots, and the actual route must be frozen and owner-approved after council acceptance.
