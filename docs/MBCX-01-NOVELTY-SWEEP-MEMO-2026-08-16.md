## 0. The composition being tested against

Six axes (read at the byte this session from design r9 / R10 spec / R13 closure): **A. Exactness gate** — conjunctive `A0 AND B AND A1` over schema-validated, identity- and hash-linked artifacts; schema-invalid output is observed failure, not missingness; no graded credit, no judge. **B. Physical boundedness** — retired instructions via Windows ETW PMC, per grid cell, 100 frozen (H,D,F) cells, preregistered max-minus-min tolerance 500,000 retired instructions across the H range at fixed (D,F), plus five absolute axes, inside a non-breakaway Job Object with signed process-tree evidence. **C. Preregistration** — whole instrument frozen at a pinned commit with hashed manifest; one terminal look at n=768; "more calls" ruled out. **D. Non-inferiority** — margin −0.10, OM floor 0.80, family error 0.05 across two streams, exact DP missingness hull over every legal completion. **E. Cryptographic reconstruction** — subjects reconstructed commit:path via git cat-file with SHA-384 + byte + LF/line verification before import; SHA-384 root over the 768 ordered candidate hashes. **F. Bitemporal obligation semantics** — three-coordinate cut; visibility and effectivity as separate executable predicates.

Honest framing caveat first: **MBCX-01 has executed nothing.** Every work below has actually run. The comparison is design composition vs executed work, and that asymmetry cuts against MBCX-01, not for it.

## (a) The closest works (all SEARCHED — opened at origin; full-text HTML additionally opened where noted)

**Lane 1 — agent/LLM memory systems and evaluations**

1. **PM-Bench** (Liu & Gabriel, arXiv:2607.12385, Jul 2026; abs + full text). Virtual-Week-derived prospective-memory benchmark: deferred intentions discharged at the right cue over seven-day schedules with cross-day obligations, rescheduling, cancellations, hidden channels. Closest published work on the SUBJECT MATTER; scoring is programmatic (Set-F1 against solver-validated due sets), not judged. LACKS: graded not conjunctive (3-of-4 due scores 0.857 there, 0 here); no physical measurement of any kind; no preregistration, CIs, or tests; no bounded-vs-oracle contrast; no pinning stated.
2. **LongMemEval** (Wu et al., arXiv:2410.10813, ICLR 2025; abs + full text). 500 questions over scalable chat histories incl. temporal reasoning and knowledge updates. LACKS: LLM-judged (gpt-4o judge, "97% agreement" = a 3% judge-error floor under every number); no cost measurement; no statistics; no pinning stated.
3. **LoCoMo** (Maharana et al., arXiv:2402.17753). Long conversations from temporal event graphs — a generator-side ancestor. Scoring/cost/statistics NOT ESTABLISHED AT ORIGIN this session (full text unopened); absence not asserted.
4. **MemGPT** (Packer et al., arXiv:2310.08560). The direct architectural ancestor of the bounded-carrier idea. LACKS: bounds the context window (an input-size bound), not the WORK to produce it from a growing history — MBCX-01's physical gate is exactly that second quantity. Metrics/statistics not established at origin.
5. **Zep/Graphiti** (Rasmussen et al., arXiv:2501.13956; abs + full text). THE genuine bitemporal overlap: t'created/t'expired vs tvalid/tinvalid — valid-time/knowledge-time separation applied to agent memory, published Jan 2025; invalidation-not-deletion; and a bounded-vs-full contrast with a cost number (2.58s vs 28.9s on LongMemEval). LACKS: two timelines not three (no journal-sequence cut coordinate; no separate visibility/effectivity predicates); LLM-judged on both benchmarks (verbatim quotes banked); latency on unstated hardware, not instructions, no tolerance, no containment, no receipt; no preregistration/CIs/equivalence; demonstrates faster, does not claim or test bounded-across-history-scale.
6. **Mem0** (Chhikara et al., arXiv:2504.19413). Closest on the bounded-vs-full ENGINEERING comparison: 91% lower p95 latency, >90% token savings vs full-context, six baseline families. LACKS: LLM-as-a-Judge headline; tokens/latency are provider/wall-clock proxies; SUPERIORITY framing (the opposite hypothesis shape from non-inferiority — failing to reject a difference is not equivalence); no preregistration; boundedness across history scale not claimed.
7. **MemoryAgentBench** (Hu et al., arXiv:2507.05257). Incremental multi-turn delivery = the append-only-journal premise; conflict-resolution/selective-forgetting territory. Scoring/cost/statistics NOT ESTABLISHED AT ORIGIN; absence not asserted.
8. **STATE-Bench** (Microsoft OSS blog, May 2026). The only work found running a DETERMINISTIC environment-state scorer alongside a judge, plus a conjunctive reliability metric (Pass^5 = success on all five runs) and token/tool-call efficiency. LACKS: headline blends exact and judged components; tokens not CPU; no preregistration/non-inferiority; baseline is no-memory floor, not oracle-memory ceiling.
9. **Du survey** (arXiv:2603.07670, Mar 2026; abs + full text). Field-scope survey 2022–early-2026; its own §5.5 verbatim: "None of the current benchmarks systematically report efficiency metrics alongside effectiveness." Independent testimony from inside the field for the cost gap. No mention of preregistration, non-inferiority, formal testing, bitemporality, or exactness guarantees in the text as read — the survey's coverage is its own claim, not proof of absence.

**Lane 2 — statistics: preregistration and non-inferiority in model evaluation**

10. **Cochran, "Progressive Disclosure… a Preregistered Ablation"** (arXiv:2607.04576, Jul 2026; abs + full text). **The single closest work on statistical composition — credited plainly.** OSF-preregistered (plan frozen at a git tag before confirmatory runs); corpus frozen at immutable git tags with a byte-identity parity gate across arms; non-inferiority margin δ=0.5 on a 0–8 composite; mixed-effects model + bootstrap CIs; 960 preregistered runs; cost measured three ways (USD, context tokens, new-token burden); result: non-inferior quality at one-third to under-half cost. LACKS vs MBCX-01: the dependent variable is an LLM judge whose own preregistered validation FAILED (human-audit Cohen's κ=0.23 vs its preregistered 0.60 threshold); dollars/tokens not CPU/counters; no family-wise correction; no missingness hull; no obligation state or bitemporal model; its comparison arm is not a full oracle.
11. **Gringras, "Safety Under Scaffolding"** (arXiv:2603.10044, Mar–Jun 2026; abs + full text). N=62,808 blinded, preregistered, equivalence-tested evaluations; OSF-registered hypotheses/model/rubrics/margins/spec-curve BEFORE data; TOST at ±2pp; Holm-Bonferroni; cluster-robust SEs; stated power; 80.9% deterministically scored; UUID randomization + hash verification of sanitized responses. On the pure inference axis AT OR ABOVE MBCX-01's declared machinery, and two-sided TOST at ±2pp is a STRICTER claim than the one-sided −0.10 margin — said plainly. LACKS: no memory carrier, no continuity claim, no cost measurement of any kind, no history-scale range; hash verification of files ≠ Merkle-rooted cohort + clean-room reconstruction.
12. **Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals"** (arXiv:2411.00640, Nov 2024). The reference "evaluations are experiments" argument; paired-test recommendation is the methodological warrant for MBCX-01's paired BC/OM design. LACKS: recommendation paper; difference-detection framing, stops short of non-inferiority; no cost axis.
13. **NeurIPS Pre-registration in ML workshops** (PMLR v148/v181, 2020–2021). The institutional precedent — protocol judged before numbers; negative results publishable. MBCX-01's enumerated valid final outcomes are that norm made executable. LACKS: a venue and a norm, not a measurement standard.

**Lane 3 — systems: bounded incremental work; cryptographic experimental integrity**

14. **DBSP** (Budiu/McSherry/Ryzhyk/Tannen, arXiv:2203.16684; PVLDB/VLDBJ). The strongest systems-side statement of the core wager: exact answers maintained by work proportional to the CHANGE, not the history — where "bounded work, exact result" is a THEOREM. LACKS: relational views, not agent obligation state; no bitemporal cut, no model in the loop; bounded-memory specifics NOT VERIFIED AT ORIGIN (recorded, not asserted); no per-cell physical measurement; preregistration n/a.
15. **Orogat & Mansour, "Is Agent Memory a Database?"** (arXiv:2605.26252, May 2026). The position IS MBCX-01's premise: agent memory as a data-management workload; correctness at the STATE-TRAJECTORY level rather than per-record — the same move as scoring the whole episode. LACKS: vision paper + feasibility prototype; no correctness metric, methodology, or cost measurement stated; no bitemporality/exactness/preregistration.
16. **Keita & Homan, "CS Conferences Should Require Nonrepudiable Experimental Results"** (arXiv:2605.08586, May 2026). The same threat model as the lane's signed receipts — a self-reported number is not evidence the reported code produced it. That the field is publishing position papers arguing for attestation in mid-2026 signals the lane's signed-receipt design is ahead of the norm, not behind it. LACKS: a proposed norm, not an executed instrument; no exactness predicate, no measurement or inference machinery.

**Lane 4 — measurement precision: how close does published best practice get?**

17. **Weaver & McKee, "Can Hardware Performance Counters be Trusted?"** (IISWC 2008; project page at origin). The direct methodological ancestor of the instruction tolerance: retired instructions as the low-variance work measure, WITH its non-determinism characterized (ASLR, environment-size perturbation, errata). ⚠ The widely-quoted variation figures (≈1.07% unprepared / <0.002% controlled) came from a snippet and were NOT verified at origin — verify from the PDF before quoting in any committed artifact. LACKS: x86 Linux not Windows/ETW; measures counter determinism as a subject, gates nothing.
18. **Mytkowicz et al., "Producing Wrong Data Without Doing Anything Obviously Wrong!"** (ASPLOS 2009; USI group page at origin — ACM DL 403'd). Measurement bias flips conclusions; 133-paper survey: none adequately consider it. The ancestor of the tolerance idea AND of the environment freeze — but its prescribed remedy is setup RANDOMIZATION, which differs from the lane's FREEZE-AND-ATTEST: a genuine methodological divergence the lane owes an argument for.
19. **SIGPLAN Empirical Evaluation Checklist** (landing page at origin). The field's canonical rigor checklist requires — on the page as read — neither preregistration, nor counter-level measurement, nor cohort pinning. (Checklist PDF unopened; "not present in what was read.") The discipline's own published standard sits below all three of MBCX-01's precision axes.
20. **ACM Artifact Review & Badging v1.1** (canonical page 403'd; EuroSys badge page quoting v1.1 verbatim used as origin — declared). "Artifacts Available" = DOI + identifier (a LOCATION guarantee); "Results Reproduced" = obtained by another team using the artifacts. None of the badges require digest pinning, preregistration, or a precision tolerance. MBCX-01 requires a CONTENT guarantee (digest-verified clean-room reconstruction before import) — strictly stronger than any badge asks.
21. **ENAMEL** (Qiu et al., arXiv:2406.06647, ICLR 2025). The most statistically careful efficiency benchmark found: eff@k with right-censoring handled, unbiased Rao-Blackwellized estimator. Treats cost measurement as statistical estimation with censoring — the same seriousness the missingness hull applies, aimed at the cost axis. Its bet (better ESTIMATOR over wall time) differs from the lane's (better PHYSICAL QUANTITY); ENAMEL's is the published one. LACKS: generated code not agent memory; no preregistration/non-inferiority indicated.
22. **Trukhina & Vashkelis, "Compress the Context, Keep the Commitments"** (arXiv:2605.17304, May 2026). Title states the thesis almost verbatim; typed source-grounded atoms with canonical identity; commitments-must-survive-compression as a first-class concern. LACKS — and the gap is wide: recall-based graded metrics (recall of commitments is not preservation of commitments); "a small diagnostic study," explicitly not a benchmarked evaluation; no proof, no bounded-work claim, no preregistration/statistics/physical measurement.

## (b) Overlaps — what was genuinely done first, credited plainly

1. Bitemporal fact modeling applied to agent memory, in production, published: **Zep/Graphiti (Jan 2025)** — the idea of bitemporal agent memory is Zep's in the agent literature and Jensen/Snodgrass's in the database literature; MBCX-01 adds the third cut coordinate and the visibility/effectivity split.
2. Bounded-carrier vs full-context with cost reported alongside quality: **Mem0 (Apr 2025)** and **Zep (Jan 2025)**. MBCX-01 is not first to ask "does the bounded thing keep up while costing less" — it asks with a different dependent variable and cost unit.
3. Preregistration + non-inferiority + a byte-identical frozen corpus at immutable git tags + cost measurement, in one LLM study: **Cochran arXiv:2607.04576 (Jul 2026)** — the closest single composition found, predating any MBCX-01 execution, in a document-retrieval domain.
4. Preregistered equivalence testing at scale with family error control in LLM evaluation: **Gringras arXiv:2603.10044 (Mar 2026)** — on the pure inference axis at or above MBCX-01's declared machinery; its two-sided ±2pp TOST is stricter than the one-sided −0.10 margin.
5. Programmatic, non-judged scoring of due-obligation discharge in LLM agents: **PM-Bench (Jul 2026)**.
6. Deterministic environment-state scoring plus a conjunctive reliability metric in an agent-memory benchmark: **STATE-Bench (May 2026)** — Pass^5 is a published conjunctive gate in this space.
7. Retired instructions as the low-variance work measure, with its non-determinism characterized: **Weaver & McKee (IISWC 2008)** — the choice of retired instructions over wall time is theirs.
8. Measurement bias as a first-class threat: **Mytkowicz et al. (ASPLOS 2009)** — the reason a tolerance and a frozen environment are defensible at all; their prescribed remedy (randomization) differs from the lane's (freeze + attest), a divergence the lane owes an argument for.
9. Bounded incremental work with exact results as a general algorithmic result: **differential dataflow / DBSP (2022–2025)** — "exact continuity under bounded work" is a known class of claim being asked in a new domain.
10. Cryptographic attestation of experimental results as a proposed publication requirement: **Keita & Homan (May 2026)** — the norm argued for publicly, independently of this lane.
11. The field's own diagnosis that cost is unreported: **Du survey §5.5 (Mar 2026)** — the gap was named five months before this memo.

## (c) Verdict

**Did someone already do it?** Every COMPONENT has a published predecessor, several recent and strong (enumerated above). **The COMPOSITION was not found in the shapes this sweep covered:** no single work among the 22 opened combines (i) a conjunctive exactness gate over a multi-node episode with identity/hash-linked artifacts, (ii) a preregistered PHYSICAL work bound in retired instructions per grid cell with a declared tolerance across a history-scale range, (iii) preregistration of the whole instrument at a pinned commit with a single terminal look, (iv) a non-inferiority estimand with declared family error and an exact worst-case missingness hull, and (v) clean-room reconstruction of every subject from cryptographically pinned objects before execution. Nearest approaches and exactly how far each gets: Cochran (iii + partial iv/v, judge failed its own preregistered κ threshold, no CPU); Gringras (iii + iv with real family control, no carrier, no continuity, no cost); PM-Bench (nearest to (i) in the obligation domain, graded, uncosted, unregistered); Zep/Mem0 (the bounded-vs-ceiling contrast with cost, judged, proxy units, no uncertainty).

**To this level of precision?** Split honestly. WHERE MBCX-01 IS AHEAD of everything opened: the exactness gate; the physical measurement (nothing in the agent-memory literature measures retired instructions at all — the field's own survey says cost is systematically unreported); the reconstruction discipline (ACM badging asks for a DOI, a LOCATION guarantee — MBCX-01 demands a CONTENT guarantee; SIGPLAN's checklist requires none of the three axes). WHERE PUBLISHED WORK IS AT OR ABOVE: Gringras's TOST + Holm-Bonferroni + cluster-robust SEs (stricter inference machinery); ENAMEL's censoring-aware Rao-Blackwellized cost estimator (a different bet — better estimator vs better physical quantity — and theirs is the published one); Mytkowicz's randomization remedy vs the lane's freeze-and-attest (an argument owed). **The load-bearing caveat: MBCX-01 has run nothing.** Precision of design is not precision of result; the correct comparative statement is that MBCX-01 SPECIFIES a stricter instrument than anything this sweep opened, not that it HAS one.

**Claim shape.** Coverage enumerated: agent/LLM memory systems + benchmarks; prospective-memory evaluation; context compression; LLM-evaluation statistics; IVM/event-sourcing; performance-evaluation rigor; cryptographic experimental integrity. Named unswept surfaces (open, not searched-and-empty): non-English literature; patents; industrial internal evals; formal-methods venues; the OSF registry directly; ACM DL / IEEE Xplore full text (403'd or unqueried); full texts of LoCoMo, MemGPT, MemoryAgentBench, DBSP (their scoring/cost/statistics recorded as NOT ESTABLISHED AT ORIGIN, never as absent).
