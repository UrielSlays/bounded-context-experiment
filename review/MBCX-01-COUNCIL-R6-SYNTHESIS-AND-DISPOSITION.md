## 3. Finding disposition

### A. State/action totality — ADOPT

Demonstrated defects:

- duplicate idempotency is ambiguous across scopes;
- ACT scope/obligation is not mapped to the obligation's distinct completion_action_id;
- invalid B proposals have two writer paths with different A1 input;
- record_handoff_response cannot journal accepted A0 or A1 calls;
- worker_handoff_v1 conflicts with the design's V2 types;
- retroactive correction wording conflicts with the linear-chain rule;
- RFC 8785 admits integers that alias in binary64;
- ordered-pair coverage can pass on disjoint objects without exercising interaction.

Adopted repair: a v2 reducer with scoped semantic idempotency, closed catalogs, a typed action executor, deterministic rejection, node-level call response journaling, safe integer domains, one bitemporal correction algorithm, and shared-entity transition vectors.

### B. Packet and presentation contract — ADOPT

Demonstrated defects:

- DecisionPacketV2, TaskPacketV2, and WorkerHandoffV2 have no closed normative schemas or renderer;
- a conforming length-only discriminator can identify an arm;
- producer explicit config can smuggle selected state or a precomputed packet;
- multi-gold cuts require A0 to guess an uncommunicated tie-break.

Adopted repair: one closed JSON Schema artifact, deterministic renderer, ID-permutation render tests, closed candidate config, and paid cuts with exactly one gold action. Frozen over-generation supplies 64 eligible cuts per motif without post-outcome substitution.

### C. Physical-work boundedness — ADOPT

Demonstrated defects:

- one dimensionless normalized slope treats bytes, records, pages, and operations as commensurable;
- work can pass by splitting across counters;
- H and D marginal slices miss a joint H-by-D interaction;
- preprocessing, index construction, background work, and open/decode accounting lack one closed ledger.

Adopted repair: a separate measurement contract with a closed counter catalog, unit-specific absolute allowances, within-unit total-charge gates, crossed H/D edge slices, registered preprocessing, and a mandatory interaction red candidate.

Rejected remedy: normalizing every intended index counter by log2(H). The counter must first state its physical unit and exact permitted range; a generic asymptotic normalization would reintroduce implementer discretion.

### D. Inference and operating characteristics — ADOPT WITH MODIFICATION

Demonstrated defects:

- 52/125 structural OC cells and 156/375 missingness-expanded cells are impossible;
- delta-only missingness completion leaves OM unspecified and can flip the scientific branch;
- exact periodic vector inputs and indexing are not frozen;
- upstream Git blob IDs are false;
- the current method's common conditional-mean applicability is not established for a motif-balanced heterogeneous manifest;
- stopping behavior and per-look branch reachability are unstated.

Adopted repair: define the target as the average conditional mean over the frozen motif-balanced manifest; pin a confidence-sequence theorem and implementation valid for varying conditional means; parameterize OC cells by feasible paired probabilities; enumerate joint BC/OM completions; freeze exact vector inputs, source blobs, imports, defaults, and environment; and state the stopping law.

Modified remedy: retain an inferential OM bound rather than replacing it with a point estimate. The inconclusive region and power/cost implications must be reported before authorization. An inconclusive answer is scientifically valid; hiding its likelihood is not.

### E. Cohort, transport, and cost — ADOPT

Demonstrated defects:

- F=16 conflicts with the scored action-envelope cap;
- exactly 384 eligible paid scenarios are required but no frozen shortfall rule exists;
- the six-call-node pair ceiling implies an unstated per-call transport reliability requirement.

Adopted repair: make F=16/64/256 deterministic resource cells, overgenerate 80 candidates per motif and freeze the first 64 eligible by hash order, and require a no-paid route soak plus authorization-facing reliability/cost report.
