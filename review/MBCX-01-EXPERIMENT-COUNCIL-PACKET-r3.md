## 2. r2 architecture under review

r2 makes the following high-level choices:

1. **Primary F versus TN package contrast.** F is a strong flat current-state/obligation store with a competent bounded materializer. TN is typed persistence plus native typed bounded materialization.
2. **TF mechanism subset.** On 25% of scenarios, the typed store is rendered through F's exact flat packet/materializer, enabling `TF-F` and `TN-TF` diagnostics without making three arms primary everywhere.
3. **Truly action-only model output.** No required provenance or state-explanation fields.
4. **Primitive public events.** Retractions refer to prior public events without repeating the displaced value.
5. **Explicit scenario maxima and mandatory retraction stresses.** Every declared update mechanism appears in every confirmatory scenario.
6. **Pre-inference budget selection.** Choose the smallest of 1,536/2,048/3,072/4,096 tokens satisfying exact-tokenizer load and pressure invariants.
7. **Direct Python runner only.** No Inspect/Harbor/framework branch in Stage A.
8. **Capability-limited reconstruction cursor plus negative witnesses.**
9. **One in-flight request and whole-pair invalidation for ambiguous post-send failure.**
10. **Exact micro-F1 scorer.** At least 30 gold-positive high-stress pairs and 20–36 lure exposures per scenario.
11. **Coherent decision boundary.** `+0.03` is the minimum worthwhile boundary; `+0.08` is the design alternative, not an observed-mean cutoff.
12. **Full-rule simulation.** Required operating characteristics cover win power, false win, kill power, false kill, and interval coverage over smooth, quantized, two-point, and rare-tail families.
13. **External 12-pair pilot.** Exact 95% upper SD formula with an SD floor of 0.20; no sample-size clamp.
14. **Explicit non-claims.** Retrieval, learned writing/controllers, compaction, and general memory remain separate.

Your job is to attack these choices, not merely restate them.

## 3. Claims to adjudicate

Return `HOLDS`, `FALSE`, or `UNDERDETERMINED` for every claim.

### C1 — The primary treatment is honestly identified

F receives every cheap flat-compatible selection/materialization operation available from its fields. TN's remaining advantages require typed persistence or typed relationships. A TN-F win is reportable as a typed package win without being mislabeled pure representation isolation.

### C2 — The TF subset is the right smallest decomposition

The 25% TF subset can distinguish typed-persistence value from typed-packet-exposure value sufficiently for an engineering decision, without adding a weak dump-everything flat arm or making all three conditions primary.

### C3 — F is strong enough to kill TN fairly

The flat reducer, single source ID, direct prerequisites, heartbeat/next-check field, stable filter/order, terminal suppression, and explicit overflow policy constitute the strongest cheap deterministic flat comparator without crossing into version chains or typed closure.

### C4 — Public events do not privilege TN

The primitive event grammar gives both reducers adequate deterministic evidence while withholding derived currentness, due-ness, closure, or answers. Mandatory retraction cases genuinely test retained history rather than a T-shaped private ontology.

### C5 — The budget contract is binding and fair

The candidate-selection algorithm cannot choose a nonbinding or impossible budget, cannot tune to model outcomes, and does not unfairly kill TN merely because typed records are denser.

### C6 — The workload envelope makes every gate executable

The declared minima/maxima are sufficient to construct maximum-load fixtures, bound the action response, cost the run, and prevent an implementer from choosing a generator width that predetermines the winner.

### C7 — The primary metric has adequate, invariant resolution

The exact micro-F1 universe, minimum 30 gold-positive pairs, lure exposure, no true-negative counting, and invalid empty scenario make `+0.03`/`+0.08` comparable across scenarios and prevent a one-item quantum from deciding the experiment.

### C8 — The decision rule is statistically coherent

The Student-t primary interval, BCa/permutation sensitivities, `+0.03` null boundary, `+0.08` design alternative, complete operating-characteristic calibration, 95% pilot-SD upper bound, SD floor, rare-tail envelope, and no clamp jointly support both win and kill decisions.

### C9 — The external pilot and cost gate are rational

Excluding the first 12 pairs is worth the extra cost because the new instrument may expose defects; the four-point pre-pilot envelope and exact post-pilot cap reveal affordability before confirmation.

### C10 — Reconstruction measures the named construct

The child capability boundary, artifact-withheld/corrupt negative, ambient-removal positive, and prefix-hash failure prove process-cold reconstruction actually depends on the arm artifact rather than replayable cursor/history state.

### C11 — Runner/transport/retry identity cannot mimic an arm effect

The direct runner, exact route/cache/transform assertions, one in-flight request, pre-dispatch-only retry, whole-pair invalidation, reserve-seed list, and 5% invalid-pair stop are sufficient and proportionate.

### C12 — The instrument remains the smallest decisive experiment

r2 has not added a framework, database platform, general lifecycle system, third full primary arm, retrieval family, learned writer, provider-variance study, or production doctrine. Every remaining moving part is load-bearing.
