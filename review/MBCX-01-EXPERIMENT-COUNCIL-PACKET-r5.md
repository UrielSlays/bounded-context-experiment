## 3. r4 architecture under review

1. **Three decisions, not one overloaded contrast.** Bounded continuity is primary; mechanism selection is nested; model usability is conditional.
2. **Three deterministic candidates.** FD derives public semantics from primitive events at read time. FC adds coarse current/terminal materialization. TN adds typed lifecycle/conflict/valid-time/provenance materialization.
3. **One semantic target.** All candidates must emit the same reference `DecisionPacketV1` under a generous envelope.
4. **No fake treatment.** Byte-identical packets produce one paid call. Candidate differences under an operating envelope must be declared as exact omissions, faults, or budget exhaustion.
5. **Obligations are hard state.** Critical due obligations, ownership, lifecycle, scope, reconstruction, and duplicate effects receive zero-failure gates rather than average-F1 forgiveness.
6. **Archive and frontier are separate.** Archive-padding twins hold the active frontier fixed; a second axis explicitly grows the live frontier.
7. **Selective same-model handoff.** Fresh B receives `TaskPacketV1`, not A's transcript, and returns a typed proposal. One deterministic writer commits or rejects it before fresh A resumes.
8. **History baselines are split.** Evidence-only history and evidence-plus-process-exhaust are distinct diagnostics.
9. **Artifact-causal reconstruction.** Sanitized child, source-directory rename, artifact swap, field mutation, and information-sufficiency witnesses remain mandatory.
10. **Bounded primary inference.** The conditional screen uses a bounded-mean empirical-Bernstein confidence sequence on `BC - OM`, a fixed `-0.10` noninferiority margin, three looks, and a hard `n = 384` cap.
11. **No paid discovery.** Deterministic stages, transport identity, statistics, and the complete cost table precede any owner authorization.
12. **Direct disposable runner.** Files, fresh child processes, and single-in-flight HTTPS; no framework, service, database, or daemon.

Attack these choices. Do not merely summarize them.

### C1 — r4 preserves the original North Star

### C3 — obligation hard gates are scientifically appropriate and executable

Zero critical loss, wrong-scope admission, stale resurrection, duplicate effect, or reconstruction drift is a valid deterministic contract, while paid model-use errors remain separately measured.

### C4 — FD and FC are the strongest cheap simple comparators

FD receives every ordinary cheap replay/index/traversal capability; FC receives the empirically motivated coarse invalidation/current-state path. TN is not favored by withholding public-event-derived semantics.

### C5 — semantic parity and unique-packet deduplication prevent a degenerate mechanism claim

Generous-envelope parity, reference-proven empty fields, exact residual declarations, and one call per unique packet prevent presentation or stochastic variation from masquerading as a persistence effect.

### C6 — archive-padding twins test boundedness without hiding active-frontier limits

Identical active worlds at 128/1,024/10,000/100,000 events, primitive-object inspection identity, and a separate 1/4/16/64/256 frontier curve can reveal archive-linear work without claiming a universally fixed kernel.

### C7 — the A→B→A handoff isolates continuity rather than model substitution

Same endpoint, fresh calls, no transcript/scratch inheritance, typed proposal, deterministic commit, idempotency, and fresh-A reconciliation test selective continuity without importing concurrent or cross-model coordination.

### C8 — the complete action universe and scorer are executable

### C9 — the Stage-1 mechanism recommendation is fair

### C10 — the paid usability screen is coherent and proportionate

### C11 — baselines, reconstruction, transport, and cost cannot choose the answer

LR/EF/XT/CO are correctly diagnostic; artifact-causal witnesses, frozen direct transport, paired missingness, and a complete preauthorization cost envelope prevent hidden state or operational censoring from driving the result.

### C12 — r4 is the smallest decisive instrument for the revised question
