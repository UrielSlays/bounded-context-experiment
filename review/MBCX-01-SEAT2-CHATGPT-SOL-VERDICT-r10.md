r10 states:

but does not specify a trial schedule or mechanism establishing that premise.

A direct counterexample shows why it matters. Under perfectly correlated trials with marginal bad-route failure probability .02:

with probability .98, every trial is clean → route passes;

No different binomial (n,c) fixes a failure of the binomial model.
