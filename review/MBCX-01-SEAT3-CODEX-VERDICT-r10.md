## Eight open questions

2. **HELPFUL WITH A NAMED FIX.** The visible/effective predicate is conceptually correct; the transition implementation is incomplete and contradictory. Fix: select the unique visible/effective head and enforce half-open, non-inverted intervals on every transition.

3. **HELPFUL WITH A NAMED FIX.** Manifest authority is useful, but exact served-byte and score identity are not closed. Fix: add expected served SHA-256 and enforce validation and causal lineage inside the scorer.

4. **HELPFUL WITH A NAMED FIX.** Job Object accounting is the right platform primitive. Fix: bind wall time, job membership, completion, I/O/external-work policy, and one candidate identity into a harness-produced receipt.

5. **HELPFUL.** The missing-completion DP was exact against independent enumeration for arbitrary tested positions and is conservative for both endpoints. No replacement is indicated.

8. **NOT HELPFUL.** The r9 runtime dependency is not merely pinned substrate; it is unnecessary attack surface that reintroduced defects. Remove the runtime inheritance.

The 456-trial route gate buys a precise acceptance decision about full six-call pair reliability—conditionally on those trials being representative and independent. It schedules 2,736 positions before retries. It does not produce scientific evidence and does not show that the model is useful.
