## 8. Attacks A–H

### Attack A — original-question and estimand drift

- **Input:** World 1: FD bounded/useful, TN and FC useless; World 2: no candidate archive-bounded.
- **Method:** Independent decision function implementing r4’s staged logic.
- **Output:** World 1 → continuity survives, Stage 2 skipped, FD recommended. World 2 → continuity falsified, Stage 2 skipped, no mechanism recommendation.
- **Interpretation:** C1 holds. TN losing does not kill bounded continuity.
- **Limitation:** Logical probe only; it does not rescue missing candidate semantics.
- **Label:** **EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED—SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES**

### Attack B — state algebra, SAT, and scored universe

- **Input:** `assert_fact(A) → correct_fact(B corrects A) → retract_fact(B)`, plus a correction cycle `A→B→A`.
- **Method:** Two independent smallest reducers:
  - restoration policy restores A after B retracts;
  - tombstone-chain policy leaves no current fact.
- **Output:** Divergent current states and therefore divergent gold: `ACT_ON_A` versus `NO_ACTION`. The correction cycle has no pinned admissibility answer. Sixteen event names imply 256 ordered type pairs, but r4 provides no executable rows.
- **Interpretation:** C2, C3, and C8 are false.
- **Limitation:** A total enumerator could not honestly be run because the table and schemas are not present. The divergent-reducer probe is the smallest executable substitute.
- **Label:** **EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED—SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES**

### Attack C — strongest-flat, coarse, and typed mechanism bakeoff

- **Input:** Nine families: contradiction, revocation, deadline change, delegation, reopen, dependency cycle, handoff death, stale proposal, and idempotency duplicate.
- **Method:** Contract-completeness audit against the pinned bytes.
- **Output:** Exact FD/FC/TN packets could not be generated without inventing legal rows, payloads, state serialization, action semantics, and an operational envelope.
- **Interpretation:** Candidate parity is an aspiration, not an executable property of r4.
- **Limitation:** No harness implementation was authorized; invented packets would not be evidence about the pin.
- **Label:** **EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED—SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES**

### Attack D — archive/frontier and hidden work

### Attack E — A→B→A and baseline challenge

- **Input:** Open obligation O1; wrong-scope proposal O2; corrected O1 proposal; duplicate proposal; deterministic commit; B death; A reconciliation.
- **Method:** Independent single-writer protocol substitute.
- **Output:** `P-wrong→REJECT_WRONG_SCOPE`, `P-good→PROPOSED`, duplicate→`NOOP`, commit→`COMMITTED`; final A sees O1 complete after B death.
- **Baseline attack:** Identical semantic objects were rendered as BC with `due_obligations[]` and OM with an unlabeled `objects[]` container. A deterministic schema-following model scored BC=1, OM=0, `delta=+1`.
- **Interpretation:** The handoff pattern itself is viable, but r4 does not define its transitions; OM can create a pure presentation false-positive.
- **Limitation:** Handoff semantics were necessarily invented as a substitute. No transcript leakage against a real runner could be tested.
- **Label:** **EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED—SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES**

### Attack F — artifact-causal reconstruction

- **Input:** Reference packet deliberately exposed to the candidate producer, which copies it byte-for-byte.
- **Method:** In-memory source-independent decoder plus ordinary, withheld, corrupt, source-rename, swapped-pair, one-field-mutation, and nonce witnesses.
- **Output:** Ordinary parity passed; withheld and corrupt artifacts failed; source-independent reconstruction survived rename; swapped artifacts swapped outputs; a one-field mutation stayed localized; wrong nonce was detected.
- **Interpretation:** All witness shapes can pass even though the artifact’s only cause was reference copying. Producer isolation is absent.
- **Limitation:** Directory renaming was simulated by source independence because no harness exists and repository writes were forbidden.
- **Label:** **EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED—SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES**

### Attack G — bounded inference and decision rule

- **Method:** Faithful transcription of `predmix_empbern_twosided_cs`, including alpha split and affine mapping `x=(delta+1)/2`; 3,000–5,000 deterministic Monte Carlo repetitions per distribution; exact binomial calculation for the oracle-floor branch.
- **Representative outputs at looks 96/192/384:**
  - Smooth high-discordance `delta=0`: estimated simultaneous coverage 99.57%; promotion 7.67%; inconclusive 92.33%.
  - Rare-discordance `delta=0`: coverage 100%; promotion 100%, mostly at n=192.
  - Asymmetric boundary `delta=-0.10`: coverage 99.97%; promotion 0.033%; otherwise inconclusive.
  - Asymmetric `delta=+0.10`: coverage 99.93%; promotion 100%.
  - Slight inferiority `delta=-0.12` with 20% discordance: failure only 0.44% by n=384; 99.56% inconclusive.
- **Oracle floor:** if a naïve smoke declares ceiling whenever observed success `<0.80` at a true `p=0.80`, exact probabilities are 45.94%, 48.56%, and 51.02% at n=96/192/384.
- **Missingness:** 4.9% missing pairs add ±0.049 worst-case uncertainty to the mean before CS width; r4 correctly requires decision invariance and stops at 5%.
- **Interpretation:** The CS family can be valid but is highly distribution-sensitive and conservative. r4 must freeze implementation details and predeclare acceptable power/inconclusive behavior. The floor requires its own confidence procedure or deterministic qualification rule.
- **Limitation:** No installed independent numerical package was executed; this was source-faithful JavaScript, not library-parity validation. That parity remains a mandatory no-paid gate.
- **Label:** **EXECUTED; SEARCHED**

## 9. WHAT WOULD YOU BUILD?

**READ-ONLY**

I would build a smaller executable experiment:

1. A machine-readable, total state-machine specification defining every event payload, legal transition, illegal error, lifecycle precedence, action set, canonical serialization, and idempotency rule.
2. Two independent executables generated or written from that contract:
   - reference reducer/oracle;

   - FD;
   - FC;

## 10. WOULD YOU DESIGN IT DIFFERENTLY?

**READ-ONLY — YES**

I would change the sequence and decision authority:

- First close the executable algebra and oracle. No workload or candidate work precedes that.
- Add FSΔ before claiming FD/FC are the strongest cheap comparators.
- Replace “strict dominance without regression” and the lexicographic winner with an owner knob: hard resource ceilings plus a Pareto set and predeclared indifference bands.
- Make the boundedness gate numerical: for fixed F, define an allowed operation envelope and reject archive-correlated slopes outside it.
- Make candidate-production taint isolation a gate, not a witness after production.
- Use a content- and presentation-matched OM.
- Freeze exactly one call graph.
- Qualify the oracle/interface with a confidence bound, not a point estimate.
- Defer LR/EF/XT/CO.

This preserves r4’s useful three-stage decomposition while removing machinery that cannot change the primary answer.
