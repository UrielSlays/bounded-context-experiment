# 8. Executed attacks

## Attack A — decision-program traces

**EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED — NOT-LOAD-BEARING.**

2. Every candidate H-linear → `FALSIFIED_FOR_FROZEN_WORKLOAD`; no Stage 2.

Result: C1 holds.

## Attack B — state algebra

**EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED — SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES.**

- Parsed all 17 event definitions and enumerated all 289 ordered type pairs.
- Correction/retraction trace produced the expected as-of knowledge and valid-time reversals.
- Cycle checks rejected A→B→A and self-dependency.
- Four-due-object mounting excluded a same-name object from the wrong scope.
- Cross-scope dependency and delegation were illegal.
- Fresh bypass: identical semantic operation and idempotency key in S1 and S2 yielded either two appends or global duplicate suppression, depending on which normative clause won.
- Commit trace yielded either one completion effect or `ALGEBRA_UNDERSPECIFIED`, because no exact transition mapping is defined.
- A duplicate proposal/commit can similarly be classified as `DUPLICATE_NOOP` or `ILLEGAL_TRANSITION` depending on unstated precedence.

## Attack C — canonical traces and comparator omission

**EXECUTED; SEARCHED where prior art bears.**

Attempted nine trace families:

1. fact correction/retraction/restoration;
2. obligation open/reschedule/complete/reopen;
3. dependency block/unblock/reversal;
4. same name across scopes;
5. delegation with immutable scope;
6. handoff crash and duplicate commit;

Every canonical packet attempt stopped before canonicalization: none of the 12 DecisionPacket fields has a normative schema definition.

No TN-only public semantic object was found; TN’s described objects are rebuildable views of the public journal. Content-addressed tries and materialized projections reduce to FC strategies in this scope.

## Attack D — boundedness arithmetic

**EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED — NOT-LOAD-BEARING.**

Axes: `H=[128,1024,10000,100000]`; `D,F=[1,4,16,64,256]`.

- H counter range 9 passed; range 10 failed.
- D range 0 passed; range 1 failed.
- Two counters with individual range 9 passed, while their sum with range 18 failed.
- A logical constant index lookup passed while physical records and bytes scaled linearly with H.
- Background reads `[0,0,0,0,64]` failed over D.

Thus the unfrozen counter ledger can select or reject a mechanism without changing its implementation.

## Attack E — arm-discriminator construction

**EXECUTED; UNSEARCHED — SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES.**

I generated 100 pairs satisfying the literal prose constraints:

- same outer keys and order;
- same action order/schema;
- same padding policy (`none`);
- OM with zero extra facts;
- BC with one to five neutral extra facts.

OM rendered at 508 bytes; BC at 604–992 bytes. A length-only classifier achieved **100% accuracy**. Equal padding policy is not equal length, and no exact discriminator, feature set, sample size, or threshold is defined.

## Attack F — producer-isolation witnesses

**READ-ONLY; UNSEARCHED — SETTLED-BY-PINNED-BYTES.**

The seven producer witnesses and taint reads could not run: the bundle contains no candidate implementation, harness, manifest, or executable config schema.

A final packet reconstructed from the journal is ordinary FC, not itself a bypass. The direct bypass is uncharged upstream preprocessing hidden in an unconstrained config or declared “logical operation.”

## Attack G — inference and scientific validity

**EXECUTED; SEARCHED.**

- Reproduced all five pinned reference vectors; maximum absolute error was below `4.9e-13`.
- A second independent implementation exhaustively agreed on all 510 binary sequences of length 1–8.
- Exercised promote, interface-fail, ceiling, inconclusive, transport-precedence, and design-precedence branches.
- Optional pattern with all zero deltas and 90% OM:
  - n=96: continue;
  - n=192: promote;
  - n=384: promote.
- Missing counts 0–7 remained promote; 8 forced transport failure for that pattern.
- Full structural grid validation found 52/125 impossible base cells and 156/375 impossible cells after missingness expansion.
- At 2% missingness, `0.02×384=7.68`: floor gives 7 and stays eligible; round gives 8 and triggers transport. The contract chooses neither.
- Full 10,000-replicate OC simulation was not run because the grid is mathematically invalid and the missing-data generator is undefined. Python/NumPy were also unavailable. This omission does not rescue the invalid grid.

## Attack H — call and transport accounting

**EXECUTED arithmetic; SEARCHED provider behavior.**

At looks n=96/192/384:

- accepted-call totals: 576 / 1,152 / 2,304;
- reconstruction calls: 192 / 384 / 768;
- with one permitted pre-acceptance retry per dispatch: 1,152 / 2,304 / 4,608.

Illustrative only—not authorization—at 8,000 input and 1,000 output tokens, `$2/M` input and `$8/M` output:

- accepted-only: 18.432M input, 2.304M output, `$55.296`;
- one retry on every dispatch: 36.864M input, 4.608M output, `$110.592`.

Current provider behavior makes the missing endpoint freeze material: caching may be automatic, routers may fall back, route metadata may be optional, healing can transform output, and a streamed 200 can fail after commitment.

2. Make the measurement ledger a machine-readable normative object, not prose.
